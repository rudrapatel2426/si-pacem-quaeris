Si Pacem Quaeris, Para Te ad Proelia Intra Te et Circa Te

“If you seek peace, prepare yourself for the battles within you and around you.”

Meaning

Peace requires more than the absence of conflict. It requires the strength to face the battles within and the challenges around us.

Authorship & Origin

This is an original Latin adaptation and formulation created and written by Rudra Patel in 2026.

It was inspired by the traditional maxim Si vis pacem, para bellum — “If you want peace, prepare for war” — traditionally associated with the Roman writer Vegetius.

The wording and expanded concept presented here are original to Rudra Patel. This is a modern formulation, not an ancient quotation by Vegetius.

Closing Thought

“Master yourself. Face what surrounds you. Protect your peace.”
